## Outcome and scope

Every guided example in every chapter has a picture of its actual prepared starting graph and a short caption explaining what to notice. Examples appear where they develop the teaching argument, with prose before, between and after examples as appropriate. Substantially overlapping short prompts and longer guided sequences become one stronger example. Rewrite or split surrounding Slovenian and English prose to support that sequence while preserving distinct learning goals.

This extends the implemented [chapter-owned guided learning contract](chapter-owned-guided-learning.md). Keep its single instructional surface, editable graph after completion, midway return, chapter context and exact-section reading/focus restoration. The owner's reference was the existing Follow the parameter card, with a graph image and caption beside its title, prompt and Guided example link. Extend that useful information to every card; final responsive composition may adapt to available space.

Scope includes Bézier curves, foundations, rational curves, splines, PH curves, parametrization, functions and calculus, and analysis. It includes all chapter entry points, catalog guidance into those examples, bilingual prose, image assets, capture tooling and relevant tests. No new mathematical feature, assessment, authoring platform, persistent progress, backend or renderer migration is needed.

## Acceptance criteria

**A1 — Exhaustive curriculum audit.** Inventory every current chapter section and its resolved guided-example entries at the implementation baseline, including short and long forms. Record existing IDs/URLs, owning section, learning goal, prepared scene, overlap decision, retained destination, intended insertion point and preview ownership. Every source entry has a disposition; every distinct teaching goal survives. Keep the matrix in this spec or a linked review, not as a reference-only task.

**A2 — Authored reading sequence.** Both chapter renderers consume explicit ordered content with stable example references. A reader encounters the relevant explanation before each example, and reflection or further explanation between multiple examples. Do not append all registry entries automatically or use array order as a curriculum. A section with one example may end with it when pedagogically suitable; multiple examples can remain adjacent only with an explicit teaching rationale in the audit. Every published example placement resolves once; repeated appearances require a documented distinct purpose. Slovenian and English have equivalent concepts and placement, even where sentences are split differently.

**A3 — Meaningful consolidation.** Merge a short prompt and a longer sequence when their concept, preparation and learner action substantially repeat. Retain distinct constructions or observations even on the same demonstration route. A merged example has coherent preparation and frames, plus an exploration opportunity where useful. Record why examples merge or remain separate; neither shared route nor identical serialized actions alone is a pedagogical decision. In particular preserve the Splines worked pair of parabolas and its derivative comparisons rather than replacing it with the general symmetric spline example.

**A4 — Actual-scene images.** Every retained chapter example card has a nonempty board image, bilingual alternative text and a brief bilingual observation caption. Capture the graph prepared by opening that exact example before learner edits; if preparation spans initial actions, wait for their board effects to settle. Include the curve and relevant construction at a readable scale. Generic catalog thumbnails, synthetic illustrations, later frames and empty boards do not satisfy this requirement. Sharing one file is allowed only when validated initial scenes and framing genuinely match; record the mapping.

**A5 — Reproducible assets.** Each preview maps a stable example ID to its exact launch URL, initial scene/frame, applicable numerical and construction checks, source revision/fingerprint, viewport and output checksum. Capture in isolated sessions with controlled state and settled rendering. Missing references/assets, stale scene mappings or failed checks fail validation. Reuse existing six images only after confirming scene equivalence and provenance. Captions distinguish observation from proof and state approximation where relevant. Graph labels may remain Slovenian under the current graph language limitation; accompanying alternative text and captions exist in both languages.

**A6 — Entry and return continuity.** Map current `example`, `activity`, `explore` and catalog `learn` entry forms to retained examples with correct chapter ownership. Preserve valid links via aliases where sensible; record any deliberate incompatibility, reason and user action before acceptance. New entry links, direct loads, refresh, language switching, completion and midway return reach the intended section and restore an existing, focusable entry. Section/anchor changes need their own old-to-new mapping. Browser-local presets and exported files remain compatible unless a separately justified change is documented.

**A7 — Readable, accessible chapters.** Preserve the elderflower identity and graph prominence. Cards expose image/caption, title, prompt and keyboard-accessible launch action with meaningful reading order. Images reserve space and remain legible without clipped controls or sideways page scrolling at desktop/projection-sized and narrow browser widths, and with enlarged text. Screen readers receive useful alternative text without redundant narration. Missing media must leave understandable text and a usable action; the shipped inventory must still have no missing media. Preserve essential keyboard and touch navigation and return focus.

**A8 — Verified complete coverage.** Reconcile the final matrix against all eight chapters in both languages: no unplaced registry leftovers, dangling references, unexplained concept loss, duplicate cards or missing previews. Record automated checks, actual board comparisons, chapter review, browser coverage and gaps at a stable revision. Implementation completion and acceptance completion are separate statuses.
