# Computational geometry construction visibility

Status: implemented and verified on 2026-11-03, following owner authorization after five screenshot mockups. This is a bounded refinement of the [graph visual style](graph-visual-style.md), informed by the [reference-calibrated audit](../reviews/reference-calibrated-graph-ux-2026-11-03/report.md).

## Scope and direction

Make the enabled polygon fan decomposition visible and simplify active highlights in interval sweep, segment intersections, monotone partition and monotone triangulation. Preserve the cream board, sage inactive geometry, ochre vertices, orange sweep, existing labels and non-color distinctions. Use a single muted blue (`#477694`) for active lines and slim current-event rings. Keep inactive edges about 2 px and active cues about 3 px; do not stack a thick brown outline around a green center. Hover and keyboard focus must remain recognizable without a broad ribbon.

Every open interval is blue. Hover/focus on intervals and segments increases the single blue cue to 4 px, so it remains distinguishable from the 3 px active state. Fan values use a small cream backing to remain readable over lattice marks.

Keep algorithms, scene actions, coordinates, counts, topology, signed-area encoding, overlay controls, interaction hit targets, routes and stored presets unchanged. Styling belongs to the actual board objects so figure exports and captured media inherit it. No renderer, lifecycle or app-shell migration is included.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With the polygon fan enabled, every nondegenerate component triangle has visible internal boundaries. The default pentagon shows exactly the two fan diagonals P1–P3 and P1–P4, with +12, +18 and +12 values readable over the lattice. Positive and negative fills retain their meaning. Toggling the fan off removes its construction; reversing order, dragging and visibility changes retain correct areas and owned-object disposal.
2. Interval-sweep and segment-intersection active lines are a single crisp blue stroke, with thin green resting segments. All intervals, segments, endpoints, events, sweep positions and reported-pair indications remain present. Status hover/focus still identifies the relevant object without a thick layered halo.
3. Monotone partition uses the same restrained blue active boundary and a small slim current-event ring. Preserve type markers, vertex labels, construction diagonals and sweep order.
4. In monotone triangulation's audited frame (Korak 4 od 7, Okvir 1 od 2), active piece boundaries are thin blue. Coincident dashed constructions use one clear construction stroke without a competing solid underlay. All actual edges, diagonals, filled triangles, stack/chain cues and the current vertex remain correct. Keep inactive polygon edges thin green and the sweep thin orange.
5. Regenerate the five affected demonstration posters and their existing motion assets from a fresh production build. Retain unaffected assets with explicit source provenance. At native card size, active strokes have no brown/green ribbon layering and the fan triangles are distinguishable. No card copy or layout changes.
   The existing capture manifest lacks the already implemented `bspline-blossom`, `geometric-series` and `conic-focal` routes. Capture those existing recipes too so the generator's exhaustive inventory remains valid. A bounded capture-validation compatibility fix is included if their actual defining objects require it; preserve their runtime geometry and keep validation non-vacuous.
6. Verify existing geometry and scene host tests, TypeScript, lint and production build. Browser checks cover the five affected routes/cards, fan toggle and reverse, representative point edit and trace navigation, the specific triangulation frame and resting state, language retaining the scene, narrow viewport and keyboard operation, and an SVG/PNG figure export. Review the construction topology and rendered hierarchy independently; record concrete evidence and coverage limits here.
