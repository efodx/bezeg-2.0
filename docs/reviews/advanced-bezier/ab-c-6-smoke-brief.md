## Checks

1. **Default scene, Slovenian, 1440×900.** Four boundaries, control points and \(S\) drawn. The interior-net readout shows the spec's four points. Corner status met.
2. **Shown surface.** Choose each value of the select. The mesh changes each time, the boundaries stay, and the interior readout keeps showing \(S\). Say what each view looks like; the corners of \(L_c\), \(L_d\), \(B\) and \(S\) should agree.
3. **Drag.** Drag an inner boundary point with the mouse: the mesh and the readout follow during the drag, not only at the end. Drag a linked corner: both boundaries meeting there follow, and the status stays met. Hold Shift or use the height switch for a vertical move as in the other patch routes, and say which one the route offers.
4. **Linked corners.** Turn the switch off, drag one end point: the status turns to mismatch, names the corner, and no text claims that \(S\) interpolates the boundaries. Quote the text. Turn it back on: the corner pair snaps to its average in one edit, and one undo-like Reset or Preset restore returns the scene. Say what actually restores it.
5. **Typed coordinates.** Choose a boundary and index, type a coordinate, apply: the point moves. Try an empty and a non-numeric entry.
6. **Camera and keyboard.** Rotate and zoom as on `/bezeg-2.0/patches/tensor`; *Ponastavi pogled* returns the fitted view. Walk the page with Tab from the top: report the order and whether every control can be reached and operated, focus is visible, and whether a control point can be moved from the keyboard as on the tensor route.
7. **200% text.** At 1440×900 and at 390×844 with the root font size at 200%: no panel text is clipped and no horizontal page overflow. Report `scrollWidth` against the viewport.
8. **Live language switch.** On the route with a mismatch present, switch SL to EN and back: every label, the status text and the corner names change, and the board does not move. Then in the lesson, mid-way: position kept, text changes, board does not move.
9. **Lesson walk as a student, Slovenian then English, 1440×900.** Walk all seven steps with the lesson's own buttons, reading the prompts and predictions. Report anything the text says that the board or readout does not show. In particular: the view shown at steps 2 and 3, the counts it quotes (12 distinct boundary control points), the numbers it reads from the interior net, and step 6, where a corner breaks. 11. **Phone, 390×844.** No horizontal overflow; the graph can be reached and a control point can be moved by touch emulation if the route's other patch demonstrations allow it. Say what you could not do.
