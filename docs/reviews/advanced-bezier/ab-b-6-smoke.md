## Findings

1. Lazy images: on first load of `/learn/advanced-bezier` the last two card images (arc-length and Coons) report `naturalWidth` 0 until scrolled into view (`loading="lazy"`); after scrolling all four are 840x520 with no failed request. This is not a broken image; a naive check at load time reads 0. The brief's "every image loads" holds once scrolled.
2. At 1440x900 the first frame of each lesson step puts the lesson panel over most of the board, and the board begins about 340 px down the page, so the curves are largely hidden until the student presses "Opazuj na grafu" or scrolls. Shared lesson runtime, not specific to this lesson; it matters for the step 3 greyscale judgement and for projection.
3. Play pace is about 0,4 s per step; the step 6 prompt needs about 54 s (134 steps at 10^-6). The lesson is correct, but students will wait about a minute with nothing to do; Next also works to reach the end.
4. The tolerance control is disabled while the lesson text of a frame is open; the steps 5 and 6 prompts say "izberi toleranco in pritisni Predvajaj" without saying to press "Opazuj na grafu" first. A student on the observation frame needs to know this. Not a defect in the numbers.
