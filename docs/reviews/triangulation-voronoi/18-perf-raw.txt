== Decision (task 18 item 6) ==
Per-cell filled curves are kept. At 40 sites the cells-only redraw is ~6 ms, of which ~4.7 ms is the exact clipCell work that a
batched curve would also need; the object overhead of 40 curves is ~1.6 ms. Full draw (cells, edges, vertices, markers) is ~9 ms
median at 40 sites per drag step, plus ~5 ms kernel build; 12-site redraw ~2 ms. First version (setAttribute on every update,
moveTo on markers, board.update) measured 173 ms median at 40 sites in the same harness and was replaced: restyle only on change,
function-coordinate markers refreshed individually.
Not measured: browser paint/compositing, real drag frame times (no browser run; jsdom script cost only). The "batched" figure above
includes board.update() of all other objects, so it is an upper bound for that alternative.
