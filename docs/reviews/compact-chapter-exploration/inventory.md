## Compact shared treatment proposal

Both readers now use one shared exploration component with a semantic kind/label, specific heading, optional bilingual instruction, optional static preview (`src`, alt text, caption), one prominent action, and a secondary action slot. It receives actions as caller-owned React nodes, so it cannot infer a destination or prepare a scene. Bézier cards pass their existing static figure and return-to-section context; family figures stay in their current section position and are not duplicated into cards. Family cards have no preview unless a specific association is authored later. Their free-exploration link is primary and any guided lesson remains a secondary link after it. Prepared activities use an explicit prepared-action label; family links continue to identify free exploration.

At desktop article width, Bézier uses a compact two-column card with a 200–240px preview rail and readable copy. The preview keeps its intrinsic aspect ratio with `height: auto` and `object-fit: contain`; cards without previews use one copy column rather than an empty rail. At widths up to 520px the preview stacks above the copy. The primary action retains a 44px target. Existing cream paper, green ink, restrained borders, and readable article typography remain the visual reference.
