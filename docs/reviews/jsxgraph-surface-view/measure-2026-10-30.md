## Interpretation

- Synchronous redraw did not remove a frame of visible latency. Draws now happen inside the pointermove task, so "next rAF" includes the draw cost and rose from about 3 ms to 16-30 ms, scaling with density. "Visible" is unchanged at about 49 ms at best (tensor 8 got worse, 34 -> 49), and 0 of 32 events show the draw in the next frame for tensor/triangular. The frame interval stays 16.7 ms, so frames are not dropped, but the draw lands about 2-3 frames after the event.
- Reduced detail helped teapot only a little: frame p95 100 -> 83 ms (about 12 fps), still about 5x over budget. Next-rAF is 88 ms, so the draw now blocks the pointermove task. 30/32 events show the draw in the next frame, but the cost per draw is the limit. Teapot "visible" worsened (112 -> 193 desktop, 128 -> 286 narrow); the metric may include the full redraw 150 ms after settle, which I did not separate.
- Tensor and triangular orbit still miss the 16.7 ms next-rAF budget in all 8 gating cells (worst 30.4 ms); Three passes everywhere (worst 3.6 ms).
