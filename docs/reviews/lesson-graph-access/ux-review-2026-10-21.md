# UX review: guided Taylor polynomial lesson

**Date:** 2026-10-21  
**Target:** /analysis/taylor?learn=1, public deployed revision unknown  
**Assessment:** The lesson offers a coherent, mathematically grounded sequence and a clear experiment prompt. On desktop the main usability cost is navigation nested inside a long, independently scrolling graph overlay; the lesson’s most useful controls can scroll out of view. The narrow layout intensifies that scrolling and leaves only part of the opening lesson visible. Ending the lesson also takes effect immediately.

## Strengths

- **Observed:** The opening explanation names the topic and explains the Taylor polynomial/error context. The lesson counter is explicit (Korak 1 od 7 · Okvir 1 od 2). The observation prompt gives a concrete prediction task with target degrees 3 and 6 before asking learners to reveal the error estimate. See [desktop entry](ux-2026-10-21/01-desktop-entry.png) and [observation prompt](ux-2026-10-21/06-desktop-observation.png).
- **Observed:** Clicking Opazuj na grafu exposes the graph and the relevant Stopnja n controls and the lower page shows general exploration guidance (PREIZKUSI IN OPAZUJ). Raising n from 1 to 3 and then 6 visibly updates both the curve and readout; the shown actual errors fall from 0,05162 to 0,0002263. Pokaži oceno napake reveals 0,0005393 and explains that the actual error does not exceed the estimate. This is useful action-linked feedback. See [n=3](ux-2026-10-21/08-desktop-degree3.png), [n=6](ux-2026-10-21/09-desktop-degree6.png), and [estimate revealed](ux-2026-10-21/10-desktop-estimate.png).
- **Observed:** Skrij navodila učne poti becomes Nazaj na navodila when hidden, making the return action discoverable. Nadaljuj učno pot advances to Korak 2 od 7 · Okvir 1 od 3; progress is retained through the tested experiment. See [hidden lesson](ux-2026-10-21/04-desktop-hidden.png), [restored lesson](ux-2026-10-21/05-desktop-reopened.png), and [next step](ux-2026-10-21/11-desktop-next-step.png).

## Findings

### 1. High — Lesson navigation is coupled to a nested scroll region

**Observed:** The lesson appears as a large panel over the graph, with its own scrollbar inside the page. Scrolling the lesson moves the explanatory content while the graph and tool panel stay put; the top Skrij navodila učne poti and Končaj učno pot controls scroll out of view. The remaining NAZAJ and NAPREJ controls sit vertically on the card edges. At 390×844, the browser page and lesson card both show scrollbars, while only the upper portion of the long opening text fits on screen. See [settled desktop reading](ux-2026-10-21/03-desktop-reading-settled.png) and [narrow entry](ux-2026-10-21/14-narrow-settled.png).

**UX judgment / risk:** This arrangement makes the lesson’s scroll and step movement compete, and can make progress/exit controls harder to relocate after reading. The edge labels are easy to overlook compared with conventional horizontal buttons, especially on a narrow viewport. This is a discoverability and orientation risk; no learner testing was conducted to establish its frequency.

**Recommendation:** Keep the lesson counter and previous/next actions in a persistent, clearly labeled footer or toolbar; keep hide/exit controls reachable while reading. On narrow screens, reduce nested scrolling by letting the lesson own page scroll or using a compact fixed navigation bar. Use conventional horizontal buttons with text (for example, Prejšnji okvir / Naslednji okvir) while preserving the counter.

### 2. Medium — The opening explanation visually competes with graph content behind it

**Observed:** In the reading frame, faint graph curves and readout text remain visible behind the lesson prose. The first view contains a long general explanation and a second explanatory section; the graph interaction is reached by advancing to the observation frame and selecting Opazuj na grafu. The card’s scrolling also clips content at its lower edge. See [settled desktop reading](ux-2026-10-21/03-desktop-reading-settled.png).

**UX judgment / risk:** The underlay adds visual noise to already formula-dense text. A learner may also need to read a substantial amount before seeing the actionable experiment, though the lesson may intentionally teach concepts first.

**Recommendation:** Give reading frames a more opaque background and break long exposition into shorter frames or a brief “what to notice” lead-in before the experiment. Keep the graph available in the observation frame, where it is central to the task.

### 3. Medium — Končaj učno pot exits immediately without a confirmation

**Observed:** From step 2, activating Končaj učno pot immediately removed ?learn=1, restored the demonstration view and the lesson-entry graph values (c=0, n=1, x=1, error 0,7183), and focused Spoznaj to demonstracijo; no confirmation or resume choice appeared. See [exit result](ux-2026-10-21/12-desktop-exit.png).

**UX judgment / risk:** The label is understandable, but its immediate effect gives no chance to correct an accidental activation or understand whether lesson progress will be retained.

**Recommendation:** Offer a concise confirm/continue choice, or make the control explicitly return to the demonstration while preserving a way to resume the current step. State whether progress is saved if that is relevant.

## Limits

This was a screenshot-and-action review relayed by the parent agent because direct browser access is unavailable in this subagent environment. The parent operated the browser; I inspected supplied screenshots and specified actions. Pointer timing and animation behavior were not assessed. I sampled the opening explanation, one observation experiment, one step transition, the exit action, and a narrow entry; I did not complete all seven steps, inspect alternate lessons/languages, or perform keyboard or assistive-technology testing. One intermediate scroll screenshot showed overprinted text, but the settled capture was legible, so I do not count that transient capture as a confirmed rendering defect.
