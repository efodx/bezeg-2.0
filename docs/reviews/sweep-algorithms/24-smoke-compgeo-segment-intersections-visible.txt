Playback takes one step at a time and stops at the end. Changing the segments returns to the start and stops playback. The sweep line is horizontal and moves downward; segments in the status are thickened, the segments of the current event are thickest (dashed while not yet in the status), the pair of neighbouring segments being tested is joined by a dashed line, and the intersection points found are marked with dots.

Events follow from top to bottom, from left to right at equal height. The status is ordered from left to right along the sweep line.
