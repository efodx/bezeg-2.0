# Bézier patch guided examples and preview map

Opening-view decision: retain the authored portrait-safe camera on a fresh workspace. Automatically fitting at mount would need a shared lifecycle policy that distinguishes fresh defaults from imported Presets and lesson restoration; that is outside this content integration. “Reset view” fits the current host. Authored lesson cameras remain explicit and fit both portrait and the figure viewport; the final triangle/join views maximize the mesh within that shared framing limit. The default catalog posters use their own reset-view recipes and were recaptured, including the triangular poster. No stored camera or route format changes.

This records authored source and figure delivery for task 13. `patchGuidedExampleRegistry.ts` combines the Basics, Evaluation and Advanced definitions; `patchChapter.ts` joins each section to its figure and localized copy; `guidedExamplePreviews.ts` resolves the separate `/src/images/learning/examples/patches/` image glob. The generated [guided-example-inventory.json](guided-example-inventory.json) contains 11 examples, 17 prepared scene steps and 51 expected rendered-owner records. Its surface expectations use independent Bernstein evaluation at the capture producer's density-16 sample positions. The test/browser/production acceptance boundary is tracked separately in [integrated acceptance](acceptance.md).

The six graph routes are `/patches/tensor`, `/patches/decasteljau`, `/patches/derivatives`, `/patches/subdivision`, `/patches/joins` and `/patches/triangular`. The lesson runtime owns their temporary scenes and restoration; no preview mounts a graph. `tests/guided-lessons.smoke.spec.cjs` has one named row per example, and probes rendered surface owners and camera/framing evidence.

The generated inventory is expected geometry evidence, not proof of a production capture or browser acceptance. All six surface clips and posters are published locally in the complete 51-clip catalog manifest. The joins catalog clip has a separate C1 description; its chapter figure shows the authored C0 kink. See [publication evidence](t13-media-publication-h26.md). Full integrated production checks, performance measurements, final review and deployment acceptance belong to task 14 and are not claimed here.
