Build a pure, renderer-free module `src/graphs/patches/tensorDerivatives.ts` that a later slice (09B2) turns into scene polylines. Acceptance:
  1. `tensorDifferentials(patch, u, v, options)` returns plain data: `point`, `su`, `sv`, `twist` (= `S_uv`), and `normal` as either `{kind: "defined", unit}` or `{kind: "degenerate", reason}` where `reason` distinguishes `"parallel"` (S_u, S_v parallel, both nonzero), `"zero-su"`, `"zero-sv"`, and `"both-zero"`; decide degeneracy with a **scale-relative** tolerance (relative to the net's bounding-box size squared for the cross product), not an absolute `> 0` test.   2. Drawing data, in world units: arrow segments for `su`, `sv`, `twist` and the unit normal scaled to a display length (a fixed fraction of the net's bounding-box diagonal; vectors are drawn at a common scale factor so their relative lengths stay truthful — state the rule in a doc comment; the normal is unit-scaled to the display length), each with a small arrowhead as two short segments; and a tangent-plane quad (four corners, `point ± a·su_hat ± b·sv_hat`-style parallelogram spanned by `su` and `sv`, sized to the display length) — omitted when the normal is degenerate.
  3. Corners: `cornerDifferentials(patch)` for `(0,0), (1,0), (0,1), (1,1)`, and a test confirming the corner identities: `S_u(0,0) = m·(P_10 − P_00)`, `S_v(0,0) = n·(P_01 − P_00)`, `S_uv(0,0) = m·n·(P_11 − P_10 − P_01 + P_00)` and the analogous ones at the other three corners.
  4. Degenerate examples as exported net builders for later Presets/lessons: (a) a bicubic with a collapsed edge (all of row 0 at one point, so `S_v = 0` along `u = 0` — the normal at `(0, v)` is "zero-sv" though a limit normal exists), (b) a net where `P_10 − P_00` and `P_01 − P_00` are parallel (normal at `(0,0)` "parallel").
