## Contract and independent review

`TriangularBezierPatch` supports degrees1–4. The flat net enumerates i descending, then j descending, with k=n−i−j. Parameters are `(u,v,w=1−u−v)`; A=(1,0,0), B=(0,1,0), C=(0,0,1). The recurrence and its full intermediate scheme use those same indices. Boundary curves are A→B, B→C, C→A and delegate to `PolynomialBezierCurve`. Issued curves retain captured control-point references and follow point edits; replacing the patch net requires requesting new curves.

Subdivision reads the full de Casteljau scheme once to form children (A,B,P), (B,C,P), (C,A,P). For child control (i,j,k), source indices are respectively `scheme[k][index(n-k,i,j,0)]`, `scheme[k][index(n-k,0,i,j)]`, `scheme[k][index(n-k,j,0,i)]`. Child coordinates (a,b,c) map to parent u/v as `(a+c*p_u,b+c*p_v)`, `(c*p_u,a+c*p_v)`, `(b+c*p_u,c*p_v)`. Strictly interior split points give positive orientation Jacobians p_w, p_u, p_v. Edge/vertex splits explicitly throw, avoiding collapsed children.

The independent mathematical reviewer checked canonical indices, recurrence, derivative differences, normalized `S_u × S_v`, elevation coefficients, child nets/maps, boundaries, lattice topology/orientation and the curved tensor equivalence tests. The interval-r lattice has (r+1)(r+2)/2 vertices and r² positive parameter-space triangles. Both halves of `S(x,y)=(x,y,xy)` agree with their exact degree-two triangular representations. No algebraic defect was found.

The engineering reviewer checked shared API conformance, coordinate/degree validation, reactive point versions, structural replacement, volatile points, density/cache keys, defensive output ownership, bounded LRU caches128/4 and maximum256 intervals, boundary curve reuse and test nonvacuity. Missing full-edge equality, changed-density and cached-invalid-coordinate assertions are now covered: three interior parameters on every edge, changed topology/coordinates after a density change, and primed evaluation/mesh/bounds throwing after NaN/Infinity edits. Handler inspection and final tests confirm each local repair.

Both handler and reviewers identified a floating-point boundary defect: `1-u-v` can be slightly negative for valid edge coordinates such as(0.8,0.2), causing evaluation and mesh5 to throw. Handler enumeration found232 of allowed densities1–256 with at least one affected lattice coordinate; powers of two8/16/256 were unaffected by this particular subtraction. The mathematical reviewer's initial claim that256 was affected was incorrect. The fix computes `sum=u+v`, accepts at most8ε overrun, and canonicalizes such overruns to `(u/sum,v/sum,0)`; otherwise w=1−sum. Negative coordinates and larger overruns still throw. All derivatives use the same canonical triple. Subdivision still requires strict interior coordinates.
