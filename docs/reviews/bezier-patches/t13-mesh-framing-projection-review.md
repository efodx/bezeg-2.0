# Task 13 mesh framing projection review

Review start: 2026-10-29 16:03:32 UTC. Scope: `projectRenderedMeshBounds` and its producer/validator tests; read-only review except this note.

No blocking correctness finding. The producer accumulates bounds from every indexed position used by triangle faces, rejects invalid indices and nonfinite coordinates, and caches local bounds against the uploaded `BufferGeometry`. A scene revision rebuild clears that cache; a camera-only reconcile retaining the revision reuses it. Camera and resize updates are applied before rendering, and framing is then recomputed from the retained local bounds and current world matrix/camera. The eight corners of the indexed-vertex local AABB enclose all indexed triangles; after the mesh world transform they form a convex box. For perspective projection, if all eight corners satisfy the clip-volume tests, the box is wholly in front of the near/far and side planes, and projected coordinate extrema over that convex box occur at its vertices. Thus the returned rectangle conservatively encloses the projected triangles for accepted captures. Cropped, depth-clipped/behind-camera and zero-area bounds are rejected by the capture validators (`guided-example-preview-api.cjs:200-207`); malformed/nonfinite data is also rejected.

Coverage gap, non-blocking: the actual teapot/subdivision fixtures (`threeSurfaceView.test.ts:578-608`) prove point-free owner publication and that fitted bounds lie in-frame, but do not independently project indexed vertices and assert each lies within the reported rectangle. Producer tests also do not exercise near/far-plane crossing, behind-camera, or degenerate mesh bounds; validator tests use synthetic `inFrustum`/rectangle values and therefore test rejection only. Add a vertex-enclosure assertion plus producer cases for those failure modes if stronger regression evidence is required.
