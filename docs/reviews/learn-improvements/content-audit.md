Scope: first backlog item in `docs/tasks/learn-improvements/README.md`, accepted by `docs/specs/learn-improvements.md` §1. Reviewed Slovenian first and English for equivalent meaning. No application content was changed. Findings below are pedagogical recommendations unless marked as errors; **no mathematical or translation errors were identified**.

## Prioritized synthesis

1. **P1, Parametrization exploration target and wording:** the weighted-knot section explains (d_i^\alpha) but links to `weighted-parameterization` (`familyChapters.ts:156`), whose graph warps a single Bézier/PH parameter with a different formula (`AlphaParamBezierCurveGraph.tsx:22–24`). The relevant spline graph is `spline-parameterization`; its implementation computes knot spans from endpoint chord distances raised to α (`bezier-spline.ts:147`, `geometry.ts:27–34`). Link the weighted-knot section to that spline graph and call (d_i) the chord length between knot-span endpoints. This is a demonstrated content-to-activity mismatch, not a flaw in the formula as a general model.
2. **P2, Foundations prerequisite:** the stated prerequisites are basic algebra and coordinates, but the chapter uses polar form with sine, cosine, and angles (`familyChapters.ts:35,50`). Briefly introduce radians and the polar form or list trigonometry as needed background.
3. **P2, Rational curves:** show the omitted algebra behind the identity proving the whole conic lies on the unit circle. It is the chapter's strongest claim and currently asks readers to accept “expansion shows” without a displayed step (`familyChapters.ts:75`).
4. **P2, Splines:** name the unit-scaled local parameters in the worked join so readers see why matching the derivative vectors establishes `C^1`, and how changing parameter scale changes that conclusion (`familyChapters.ts:106`).
5. **P2, Analysis:** state the uniform-convergence quantifiers once in the chapter. The current “same index threshold for all `x`” is accurate, but a short `for every ε > 0, there is N such that…` formulation would make the stronger condition usable (`familyChapters.ts:222`).
6. **P3, Functions and calculus:** include a compact tolerance/neighborhood formula alongside the prose definition of continuity, connecting the verbal account to the notation learners meet later (`familyChapters.ts:182`).
7. **P3, PH curves:** expand the worked example by integrating `x'=1-t^2, y'=2t` to an explicit position curve, then state its initial point. This would connect the velocity calculation to the promised position curve (`familyChapters.ts:130`).
8. **P3, Parametrization:** explain why α values such as 0, 1/2, and 1 are useful modeling choices and that the weighted allocation is a heuristic for segment timing, not an arc-length parametrization (`familyChapters.ts:154–158`).
9. **P3, Foundations:** add a transition explaining how the two demonstrations serve the later curve chapters: Bernstein weights combine points, while complex multiplication transforms points (`familyChapters.ts:38`).
10. **P3, Bézier:** consider trimming or varying the repeated post-activity recaps; several immediately restate the preceding paragraph (`bezierChapter.ts:26–27, 32–33, 42–43, 61–62, 70–71`).

The Parametrization target is a confirmed content-to-activity defect; the Foundations prerequisite gap and the remaining items are teaching improvements. Bézier and Splines are also more readable when their worked examples explicitly distinguish local constructions from the prepared higher-degree graph examples.
