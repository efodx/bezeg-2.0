## What to build

- Model module (e.g. `src/analysis/rationalFunctionModel.ts`): numerator and denominator zero lists (each total degree ≤ 5, as S3), `k` of each or one overall `k` (state your choice), and the remainder probe `x` with a range. Issues as data: those of S3, plus denominator without zeros allowed (a polynomial), plus an identically zero denominator impossible by construction (`k ≠ 0`).
- Graph `RationalFunctionGraph.tsx`: curve via `plotPieces` (pieces split at poles and holes; never a segment across a pole; acceptance 3). Holes as open circles at \((x, \text{limit})\). Poles: dashed vertical asymptotes with a text label (\(x=2\)). Horizontal or oblique asymptote dashed with a text label (\(y=2\), \(y=x+2\)); none when \(\deg p > \deg q + 1\), stated in text. Dragging a numerator zero onto a denominator zero makes a hole, never a pole (acceptance 1), announced in the live text; dragging it off again restores the pole. Pole multiplicity readout with whether the sign changes (odd/even).
- Readouts: the rule as \(p/q\) in product form, reduced form when a factor cancels, holes, poles, the asymptote, and for the oblique/horizontal case the division \(p/q = \text{quotient} + r/q\) with the remainder term \(r(x)/q(x)\) at the probe \(x\) (a slider; acceptance 5 of the steps: the remainder shrinks). Do not claim the graph never crosses a horizontal or oblique asymptote (acceptance 5).
- Examples (choice control and the lesson's `function-choice`): `oblique` \((x^2-1)/(x-2)\) (default), `hole` \((x^2-1)/(x-1)\), `horizontal` \(2(x-1)(x+1)/((x-2)(x+2))\), and one with a double pole for step 6 (state it).
