Playback takes one step at a time and stops at the end; it is stopped when opened. A step is one event: the sweep line moves down, the beach line is drawn approximately (parabola arcs), dots on it are the breakpoints of the arcs, and lines from the vertices to them are the parts of edges traced so far. A scheduled circle is solid with a diamond at its lowest point; a cancelled circle (a false alarm) is dashed with a cross at its lowest point. The event of the step is thickest and marked in the list too: a ring on its site, or its circle with a ring on the new vertex.
