# Wave 3 smoke brief (P1 playback variants, D1 reveal button and angle slider)

Same frame as `w2-pa-smoke-brief.md` (role, setup reading, evidence and return rules); read it for those. Differences:

**Preview:** production build of commit `d0a72a48` (plus any repair commit named in the dispatch), served by the handler from the clean worktree with `just preview 4327` at `http://localhost:4327/bezeg-2.0/`. Do not start or stop it.

**What changed** (spec `docs/specs/shared-graph-controls.md` decisions 56–60):

- **Group A, playback (P1).** Routes `/bezier/decasteljau`, `/bezier/decasteljau2`, `/rational/rational-decasteljau`, `/bezier/arc-length`, `/curve-geometry/parametric`. Playback is one shared group (`.inspector-playback`, buttons with `data-action` `play-pause` and, where present, `reset`; `play-pause` carries `data-playing`). De Casteljau: Play/Pause toggle and Reset (the old Stop; Replay is gone); the `t` readout shows two decimals with the language separator (`t = 0,50` in Slovenian, `t = 0.50` in English), moves while playing and is not announced while playing (`[data-readout-live]` stays unchanged during play). Pause on edit: while playing, dragging a control point, pressing on the board, moving the `t` slider, or adding/removing/typing a control point in the points panel pauses the run; toggling a display switch does not. Arc length: Play/Pause in the shared group; with reduced motion emulated (`page.emulateMedia({reducedMotion: 'reduce'})`), Play stays focusable, is `aria-disabled`, and pressing it shows the reduced-motion reason once. Parametric: the roll button reads "Predvajaj"/"Play" and "Premor"/"Pause"; moving the parameter slider or radius pauses the roll; reduced motion blocks Play with the reason.
- **Group B, reveals, switches and angles (D1).** Routes `/analysis/taylor`, `/analysis/darboux`, `/analysis/mean-value`, `/analysis/sequence`, `/complex-multiplication`, `/complex-roots`, `/linear-map`, `/simson-line`, `/inversion`, `/inscribed-angle`. Reveal buttons (`[data-revealed]`) look and work as before: the label swaps Show/Hide, the board shows or hides the result, there is no `aria-pressed`. Linear map: probe, curve and square are three switches (`role="switch"` or checkbox switch) that show and hide those board objects. Simson, Inversion, Inscribed angle: the angle slider with degree field (0–360, arrows step 1, Shift or Page step 10, wraps at 360, typed degrees commit on Enter).

**Per route, five checks** as in the pa brief: (1) loads, board and inspector, no console errors; (2) each changed control changes the board or readout as named above; (3) one unavailable reason (Group A: Reset at start, Play at end or under reduced motion; Group B: none needed, mark n/a); (4) keyboard: Tab to the control, operate it with Space/Enter or arrows, visible focus; (5) language switch SL→EN→SL keeps the scene and relabels the control. On two routes per group also 390×844 with touch emulation: the controls are reachable and the playback/reveal buttons are at least 44 CSS px high.

Evidence: `docs/reviews/feature-streams/controls/w3-smoke-<group>.md`; screenshots under `.command-logs/w3-smoke-<group>/`. Return ≤300 words: route × check table, failures first with repro, coverage gaps.
