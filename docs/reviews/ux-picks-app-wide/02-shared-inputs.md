# Shared inputs and a declared parameter (task 02, slice 2)

Not wired in this slice; the presenter work in tasks 04 and 05 decides how the inputs read a `ParameterDeclaration`. Today every graph hands `Slider` (`src/inputs/Slider.tsx`) its presentation and its binding as separate props: `min`, `max`, `step`, `bigStep`, `decimals`/`fieldDecimals`, `cyclic`, `grid`, `hideBounds`, `formatValue`/`valueText` (display), `customText` (label), `help`, `describedBy`, `disabled`, `unavailableReason` and `rejectedHint`, plus the binding `value`, `onChange` and `onTypedChange`. `CountSetter` (`src/inputs/CountSetter.tsx`) takes `label`, `n`, `onPlus`/`onMinus`/`onSet`, `step`, `bigStep`, `decimals`, `min`/`max` (number or getter), `typedMin`, `minReason`/`maxReason`, `minusLabel`/`plusLabel`, `help`, `describedBy` and `rejectedHint`. A declared parameter would replace the presentation props (label, range, step, decimals, help, cyclic, grid, bounds text, step labels and the min/max reasons) with one declaration read, and the binding props (`value`, `onChange`/`onSet`/`onPlus`/`onMinus`, the rejected hint) with the arbiter key's reader and validated setter (task 01); availability (`disabled`, `unavailableReason`) would come from the arbiter. The remaining props, `describedBy` and the display formatters, stay input-local until a case shows they belong in the declaration.
