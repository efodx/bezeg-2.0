## Where I would get lost or quit
- S3 okvir 1: "teža točke" appears with no clear link to the hull question. I have to wait two frames to see why.
- S3 okvir 2: "približno enako razdaljo, enega za drugim" is fiddly. The 3:1 ratio is hard to measure by eye.
- S4 challenge: the crossed polygon is a nice surprise, but no step tells me what it is for.

## Top 3 fixes
1. S2 okvir 1: ask me first, "Kje na grafu bo krivulja, če premaknem točke?", and name the hull only after I try. I would rather find the rubber band myself than be told.
2. S3: cut to one idea per frame. Put the weights in okvir 2 only, and move "uteženo povprečje" earlier or drop it. As it stands, five new terms arrive in one step.
3. S4 okvir 2: close with a forward-looking line in the lesson itself, for example "Kaj bi se zgodilo z več kot štirimi točkami?". That gives me a next step and uses the curiosity the lesson built.
