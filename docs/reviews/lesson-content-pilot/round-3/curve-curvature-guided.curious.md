## Where I would get lost or quit
- S4 okvir 2: "Potem poskusi še s četrto točko" says nothing about why. The result is only a negative ("ne vpliva"), which is a little anticlimactic.
- S5 okvir 2: "poišči točko, kjer krožnica izgine" needs fine slider control. The slider step may be too coarse to land on t=0,5.
- S6 challenge: "najmanjša ... spremeni v premico" asks two things at once.

## Top 3 fixes
1. S1 hidden text: drop "približno 11-krat večja" or hide it until after I read both values. As written it spoils my own measurement. I would like to find the ratio.
2. S4: ask "Katera točka bi morala biti zraven, da bo začetek bolj raven?" before the drag, so I commit to a prediction. The "četrta točka ne vpliva" result then has some surprise.
3. S6 okvir 2: end with a forward-looking question, for example how the road transition curve from S5 would look as control points. That gives me a next step and ties back to the road hook.
