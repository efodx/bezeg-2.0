## Where I would get lost or quit

- Step 4 frame 1: "Prvi dve ležita na osi \(x\), tretja je nekoliko višje" is a list of coordinates to hold in my head while looking at a graph. I'd start skimming.
- Step 5 frame 1: this is where I would quit. Six steps is long, and this frame has a new curve, sign meaning, a question, and a road-engineering aside.
