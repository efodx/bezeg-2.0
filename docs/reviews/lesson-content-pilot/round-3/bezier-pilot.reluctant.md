## Where I would get lost or quit
- I would not quit before step 3. Steps 1 and 2 are short and each has a clear thing to drag.
- Step 3 frame 1: "Moč vpliva imenujemo teža točke" is the first piece of vocabulary that feels like school. I would sigh, but the guess task keeps me.
- Step 3 frame 3 is optional and collapsed, so I would skip it. This is the right design.
- Step 4 frame 2 is where I would stop if I were going to. After the answer I have what I need, and "zamenjaj mesti" is extra work with no stated reason.

## Top 3 fixes
1. Step 3 frame 2: the "primerjaj, za koliko se premakne" task is hard to do by eye. Give a visible number or trace, or reduce it to "kateri premik se bolj pozna?". Right now I can't tell if I got it, so I'd just open the hidden answer.
2. Step 4 frame 2: say why the challenge matters ("Ali velja pravilo tudi, ko se poligon prekriža?"), and follow it with a one-line recap of the three facts. That gives me an ending, not a trailing puzzle.
3. Step 2 frame 1: split or trim the two definitions, and add a one-word action ("Poglej ovojnico") so I'm not reading with nothing to do.
