## Where I would get lost or quit
- Step 1: long, but the guess is easy so I stay.
- Step 2: tangent, normal and φ in one paragraph; I start skimming.
- Step 4: I quit here. A new parabola, a formula with x'y''−y'x'', and a "2×, 5× or 10×" detour from a curve I was just given.
- Step 5 (formula with n−1/n · h/a²): I would never reach it.

## Top 3 fixes
1. Cut steps 4 and 5 or make them optional; seven steps for one idea is too many. Step 4's parabola re-teaches what step 3 already showed.
2. Shorten the step 1 paragraph to the question plus one action; move the engineer line to the intro.
3. Introduce φ, T and N one at a time, or drop φ and let curvature be the size of the circle.
