## Where I would get lost or quit
- Step 1: bored; title, intro and text all say the same thing.
- Step 2 frame 1: formula and Bernstein-weights sentence before I have seen the hull.
- Step 4: nothing to predict; "ne njihovo aritmetično povprečje" gives the surprise away.
- Step 6: "Izberi lastnost" is vague.

## Top 3 fixes
1. Open step 1 with a puzzle: where does the curve go relative to these four points? Let me guess, then show it.
2. In step 4 ask me to predict B(1/2) (is it the average, (0,0)?) before the calculation; (0,1) versus (0,0) is the best hook here.
3. Replace step 6 and the ending with a recap of the three properties and one concrete challenge (make the curve leave the hull; find out why you cannot).
