## Where I would get lost or quit
- Step 1, text: the formula with b_i^r indices.
- Step 2: L(u)=B(tu), R(u)=... with a new letter u.
- Step 3: five fractional points and the reversed left/right order.
- Step 5: "drsnik sega le od 0,01 do 0,99" feels like a footnote, not a lesson point.

## Top 3 fixes
1. Start with a puzzle: "Ali lahko eno krivuljo zamenjaš z dvema in se oblika ne spremeni?" Pay it off in step 4.
2. Step 1: drop the index formula; show the scheme and ask me to count rows. Introduce u only if needed.
3. Step 3: colour the left/right control points on the graph and let me read them off, instead of listing coordinates.
