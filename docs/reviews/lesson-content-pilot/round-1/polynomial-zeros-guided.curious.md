## Where I would get lost or quit
- Intro: a giant paragraph of rules before I touch anything.
- Step 3 frame 1: the aside about x=-1 distracts.
- Step 4: "Do njega pridemo tudi iz prejšnjega primera" is hard to follow; merging is introduced by prose, not by action.
- Step 6: a y-intercept formula feels like a tangent from the main idea.

## Top 3 fixes
1. Replace the intro with a question ("Zakaj se graf pri eni ničli prebije skozi os, pri drugi pa odbije?") and withhold the rules until I have seen examples.
2. Make steps 2 to 4 predict-then-reveal; one idea per frame; let me do the merge.
3. End with a summary (odd crosses, even touches, higher is flatter) and tie step 6 in or drop it.
