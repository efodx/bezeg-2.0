## Where I would get lost or quit
- Step 1: a definition, then endpoints that are plainly visible. Too trivial.
- Step 2: "Bernsteinove uteži" appear unexplained.
- Step 4: numbers on a graph I cannot probe; I want to drag a point and watch B(1/2).
- Step 5: new curve, same weights; I do not know what I am meant to discover.
- Step 6: "Izberi lastnost, ki jo boš preveril" with no goal or challenge.

## Top 3 fixes
1. Open with a puzzle: "Premakni P_1 in P_2: kam krivulja ne more?" Let me guess the hull before it appears (steps 1-2).
2. Make steps 4/5 predictive: guess where B(1/2) lies, then reveal the weights; let me drag and watch it.
3. Close with a recap of the three properties and a concrete challenge in step 6 and Konec.
