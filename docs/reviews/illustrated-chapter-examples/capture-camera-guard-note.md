# Guided-example capture camera guard

The first capture failure was the sequence-convergence epsilon band: `SequenceGraph` draws a horizontal curve whose x coordinates are the board's left, center and right edges (`src/graphs/functions/SequenceGraph.tsx:289-295`). `presentBoard` changed those samples from `[-4.9, 19.05, 43]` to `[-2.52, 9, 20.52]` while y remained `-0.25`. The old exact comparison reported a geometry mutation; the failure is in `.command-logs/handler-captures-identity-fixed.log`.

The next failure was `foundations.complex-plane.complex-multiplication`. Its two visible complex argument arcs use `radius = min(radiusPx / board.unitX, 0.8 * modulus)` (`src/graphs/complex/ComplexMultiplicationGraph.tsx:307-323`), so their user-coordinate radii change with the camera while their angles and shapes stay fixed. The observed board unit scale changed from `67.4444` to `52.0833`; the affected curve samples scaled by about `1.2949` and `1.2263` respectively. This is not curve-order resorting: the unchanged first two curves remain first, and both changed arrays follow the two arc slots.

The guard now records board bounds and x/y unit scales. It permits a changed curve only if it either spans the old and new viewport horizontally at an unchanged y, or is a sampled origin-centered circular arc that remains the same shape with uniform scale between the cap-bound factor 1 and the fixed-pixel camera scale factor. The second rule requires isotropic camera units and checks every sample. Point coordinates, curve count, curve shape, arc angles, and changes outside those camera-scaled cases still fail. Rejection errors now include the example id, before/after samples, unit scales and bounds. All guard helpers are included in the presentation fingerprint.

Capture also sets `.guided-lesson-layer` to `visibility:hidden` before camera framing, verifies exactly one layer is hidden, and records the capture-only treatment in manifest metadata. It does not click lesson controls or change the frame or graph state.

Evidence: the original handler capture logs provide red cases for the band and arc. A focused read-only Playwright reproduction of the exact failing route passed the revised guard on actual before/after samples and wrote no PNG or manifest. `node --test scripts/capture-guided-example-previews.test.cjs` passes 5 tests, including band and cap-bound/fixed-pixel arc cases plus deliberate geometry mutations. Focused lint passed after the revision. The handler owns the full capture and browser PNG review.
