Graphs and captures: pilot

Package D of the site walkthrough (G01 to G07). Three examples, one of each kind. Left is what the catalog shows today, right is the proposal. The clips play in a loop, as on the catalog cards. Nothing here is on the public site.

What needs your answer.

  1. Capture text. Proposed rule: a catalog capture carries no words, because the same pixels are shown in both languages. It may keep axis numbers, symbols that name a drawn object, one formula line, the number the motion produces (such as r) and, in a few cases, weights. The spec lists a verdict for each of the 118 captures. Is the rule right, and is "keep r" the kind of exception you want?
  2. The three directions below. Approve, or say what to change.
  3. Graph typography (G01). Proposed: Literata, the site's title face, for plain text on every graph, set in one place; mathematics stays MathJax. Not applied here yet. Today graphs mix three faces, one of them Arial.
  4. Convexity clip (G06). It skips because it was built from four held states. Proposed: a smooth sweep of the tangent point, dropping the staged reveal of the second derivative.

1. Correlation and regression line: a weak graph

Changed in the graph: residuals are solid and heavier, the line label uses the board serif on a plate instead of Arial. Changed in the capture: axes and grid are kept, the title and the line label are removed, r stays. The points, the line and the numbers are the same. Open the live graph.

2. Envelope: capture text

The two status lines are removed from the capture and the frame is fitted to the construction, which had been pushed into a corner to leave room for the text. The live graph is unchanged. Open the live graph.

3. Subdivision curves: green points on a green curve

Changed in the graph: the points you drag are ochre, as control points are everywhere else; the refined vertices are blue with a light outline; the Chaikin limit curve, when shown, is violet so that it does not share the vertices' blue. The status line is removed from the capture. Open the live graph.

4. Convexity and concavity: a clip that skipped (G06)

Before, the clip held four states and jumped between them. After, the tangent point sweeps smoothly left, then right past the end of the interval, as the lesson asks, and back. Open the live graph.
