# 09 — Orientation and polygon-area graphs

Status: planned. Source: [spec A1](../../specs/convexity-hulls-lessons.md#a1-orientation-of-three-points) and [A2](../../specs/convexity-hulls-lessons.md#a2-polygon-area-and-picks-theorem), [§2 point-set graph](../../specs/convexity-hulls-lessons.md#2-draggable-point-set-and-polygon-graph), [batch-wide gate](../../specs/convexity-hulls-lessons.md#batch-wide-acceptance-gate). Kind: F2a, engineering + UI/accessibility review (mathematical review of the readout adapters). Depends on 05 and 07. Batch 1, not gated: no route, catalog entry, Demonstration, Preset key registration or lesson actions (F2b/F3, after `G-REG` and Platform A9a).

Two subclasses of `PointSetGraph` under `src/graphs/compgeo/`, using its documented overlay hook (`overlayIds`, `overlayLabel`, `drawOverlay`; module comment in `PointSetGraph.tsx`). Do not change the base interface; if a subclass needs a base change, keep it minimal, document it in the module comment, and report it.

1. **Orientation graph (A1).** Point-set mode limited to three points; default \(p=(0,0)\), \(q=(4,0)\), \(r=(3,2)\). Overlay `turn`: line \(pq\), the triangle \(pqr\) shaded by sign, and turn text. A readout outside the board (`role="status"`) shows the exact `orientValue` and "left turn" / "right turn" / "collinear" in words (colour is never the only signal). With fewer than three points it says what is missing. Put the value-to-readout mapping in a pure, unit-tested adapter.
2. **Polygon-area graph (A2).** Polygon mode, closed; default the hull pentagon. Overlays `fan` (triangles from vertex 0, each labelled with its signed doubled area or sign) and `lattice` (boundary and interior lattice points with distinct marker shapes, not colour only). Readout from task 07's result kind: doubled signed area, area, orientation, \(B\), \(I\), and the Pick comparison for simple polygons; for non-simple ones the label "signed sum of winding number times area" and no Pick; for open or \(n<3\) a message. A **Reverse order** control (add a pure `reverseVertices` to `pointSetModel.ts` if none exists). Vertices stay on lattice nodes (already guaranteed by snapping; assert it).
3. Locale keys in one block per graph in `sl.ts` and `en.ts`, same position in both, after the point-set graph's block; Slovenian terms from the spec's term table; `t()` per AGENTS.md.
4. Tests (host tests in the style of `PointSetGraph.host.test.tsx`, plus unit tests for the adapters):
   - A1: \(r=(3,2)\) shows \(+8\) and left, \((3,-2)\) shows \(-8\) and right, \((2,0)\) shows \(0\) and collinear reached by arrow keys only; swapping any two points negates the value; over a small grid of \(r\) positions (for example \([-3,7]\times[-3,3]\)) the displayed sign equals `orient`. Readout correct after a language switch and after a scene change.
   - A2: both default polygons show the task 07 numbers; Reverse negates the signed area only; the crossed quadrilateral shows the winding label and no Pick; overlay objects are removed when hidden and on unmount (exact object counts, as in task 05).
   - Observed failures under two deliberate mutations.

Checks: `just unit src/graphs/compgeo src/compgeo --maxWorkers=2`, `just lint src/graphs/compgeo src/compgeo`, `npx tsc --noEmit --pretty false`, production build (locale change). No browser smoke until F2b gives the graphs a route.
