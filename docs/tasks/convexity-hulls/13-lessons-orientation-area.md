## Decisions (handler 12, 2026-11-01)

- **Predict before reveal needs a `values` overlay.** The readouts outside the board (`data-orientation-readout`, `data-area-readout`) and the numeric board labels (fan triangle values, the turn text) would give the answer away in the predict steps (A1 step 2, A2 step 2). A seventh name, `values`, joins `POINT_SET_OVERLAY_NAMES`. The orientation and polygon-area graphs own it, it is on by default, and it appears in the overlay switch. When it is off, the readouts show a neutral localized "values hidden" sentence and the board shows no numbers; shading (`turn`, `fan`, `lattice`) is unaffected. Predict steps switch it off and reveal steps switch it on. Alternative: a separate lesson-only readout action. Rejected because it would widen the action vocabulary and hide state that the learner cannot switch back.
- **Interim chapter owner.** Until the convexity chapter (F4) exists, `orientation` and `polygon-area` are owned by `bezier-curves` / `geometric-properties` (the convex-hull property, which the A1 prerequisites cite). F4 moves them.
