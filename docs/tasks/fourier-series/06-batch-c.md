# 06 Batch C: epicycles (F2a–F6)

Gates: `G-FN` (F2a), `G-REG` (F2b–F6). Depends on 02 and 05 (Batch B released). Decisions: [ADR 0034](../../adr/0034-fourier-series-models-handles-and-closed-loop.md) §3–4.

1. **Measurement first.** Build throwaway scenes for the options in ADR 0034 §3 (\(N=10,25,50\); traces of 400 and 2000 samples; change \(N\) and drag a vertex). Record frame times and the threshold in a new ADR. No drawing code is merged before that ADR.
   *Measured 2026-11-05* with `scripts/epicycle-perf.cjs`: the arm chain and all circles as two JSXGraph curves pass (1.5 to 3.6 ms per playback step or vertex drag, under 6 ms p95 per N change at N=50, 2000-sample trace); one object per circle and arm does not (18 to 22 ms per N change). See [06-epicycle-measurement.md](../../reviews/fourier-series/06-epicycle-measurement.md) and [ADR 0040](../../adr/0040-epicycle-rendering.md).
2. **F2a:** the epicycle graph on the measured approach: closed loop with draggable vertices or controls (4–8 spans), arms and circles, trace, original curve, \(N\) control capped at 50 with a visible hint, \(|c_k|\) table, text status, parametrization switch. Playback is stopped by default and pauses on edits, exit, import and unmount.
   *F2a accepted 2026-11-05* (brief [fourier-06-f2a.md](../../reviews/feature-streams/functions/briefs/fourier-06-f2a.md)): `src/analysis/epicycleModel.ts` (the model owns each vertex once, because `validateLoop` compares only the first and last point; examples `circle`, `diamond`, `uneven`, the last because all symmetric examples have equal chords) and `EpicycleGraph` (unmounted, no route). `traceSum` now sorts the chain once per trace, not once per sample, with equal results. Engineering and mathematical reviews ran; repairs: transient time and playback across scene replacements, the largest term over \(k
e0\) only, closed drawing of the loop and whole trace, the error labelled as sampled, the play button semantics.
   *Moved to F2b, which has the route:* ADR 0040's re-check of control-point drags and the host's update cadence, including the per-frame copy of the trace slice, and the on-screen check of large radii with 48-segment circles.
3. Presets: loop, \(N\), parametrization; all validated.
   *Done with F2a:* `epicyclePresetCollection`, not yet in the Demonstration map.
   *F2b stopped 2026-11-05, owner decision needed:* drags on the real route miss the ADR 0040 threshold (p95 33 to 50 ms, about 30 % of drag frames over 25 ms); see [Cause of the drag miss](../../reviews/fourier-series/06-epicycle-measurement.md#cause-of-the-drag-miss). The route is not on master.

All seven cases meet the threshold in force ([re-measurement](../../reviews/fourier-series/06-epicycle-measurement.md#re-measurement-under-the-relaxed-threshold)); sustained 60 Hz pointer input and the classroom device stay unmeasured.
