# 06 Homepage elderflower

Source: [browser annotation 10](../../specs/annotated-ui-follow-up.md). Builds on the [homepage visual flair spec](../../specs/homepage-visual-flair.md).

Refine the existing decorative SVG into a compact elderflower sprig with clearer flower clusters and less exposed stem. Compose it within the introduction. Give it its own space beside the intro on wide layouts, reduce or crop it deliberately on tablet, and use a small accent or omit the decoration where it competes with the copy on phones. Keep the sprig visually subordinate to the title and the curve preview.

Review the full first viewport at 1440px, around the 820px and 700px layout transitions, and at 390px. Check both languages and enlarged text. The graphic has a deliberate crop, stays clear of the heading, buttons and preview card, adds no horizontal overflow, and remains decorative for assistive technology. Keep the homepage SVG and its positioning separate from the catalog artwork.
