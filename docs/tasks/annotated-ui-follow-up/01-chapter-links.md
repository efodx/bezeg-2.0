# 01 Chapter links

Source: [browser annotations 1, 4 and 7](../../specs/annotated-ui-follow-up.md). Builds on [chapter wayfinding](../../specs/learn-navigation-redesign.md).

Give the Learn return in `ChapterTrail` the treatment of secondary navigation, with a clean leading arrow and space between the trail and the chapter title. In the family chapter prerequisites, present related chapters as a compact, clearly separated list of links. Use the reader palette, a consistent arrow cue and visible focus. Let long titles wrap within their link targets.

Replace the visible ¶ in both chapter templates with a quiet chain-link icon. Reveal it on heading hover or keyboard focus and keep it discoverable on touch. Give it the current localized section link name and retain the existing hash, link behavior and focus destination.

Check both annotated chapters at 1440px and 390px, including enlarged text and both languages. Follow the Learn return to the correct chapter row, open the B-splines prerequisite links, and activate a section permalink by keyboard and touch. Reload the resulting URL directly and verify that it still lands on the correct heading.
