# 8. Taylor approximation

**Source spec:** [Analysis guided lessons](../../specs/analysis-guided-lessons.md) — B4

**Dependencies:** Batch A released ([04](04-batch-a-release.md))

**Status:** implemented, not released

## Part A — mathematics (`src/analysis/taylor.ts`, `taylorModel.ts`)

- Named examples with closed-form Taylor coefficients (no symbolic engine):
  - `e^x`: `e^c / k!`;
  - `sin x`: the derivative cycle `sin, cos, -sin, -cos` at `c`, divided by `k!`;
  - `1/(1 - x)`: `1 / (1 - c)^(k + 1)`, defined for `c < 1`, series converges for `|x - c| < 1 - c`;
  - the flat function `e^(-1/x)` for `x > 0`, `0` for `x <= 0`, at the fixed center `0`: all coefficients `0`.
- Degree `n` from `0` to a documented limit (for example `10`); center and evaluation point in documented per-example domains (`1/(1 - x)` keeps both below `1`; the flat example keeps the center at `0`).
- `T_n(x)`, signed error `f(x) - T_n(x)`, absolute error.
- Lagrange remainder bound `M |x - c|^(n+1) / (n + 1)!` with `M` a justified bound of `|f^(n+1)|` between `c` and `x`: `max(e^c, e^x)` for `e^x`; `1` for `sin`; `(n + 1)! / (1 - max(c, x))^(n + 2)` for `1/(1 - x)`. No bound for the flat example (state why).
- Model: `{example, center, degree, x, boundVisible}` validated by the parser (reject, never clamp).

## Part B — graph (`src/graphs/functions/TaylorGraph.tsx`)

- `f` and `T_n` distinguishable by pattern and label; draggable center and evaluation point with number fields; degree control (field or stepper); example select.
- Readout: `T_n(x)`, `f(x)`, signed and absolute error, and (after **Show error bound**) the bound with its hypothesis; for `1/(1 - x)` the convergence interval `|x - c| < 1 - c` is indicated.
- Keep finite-polynomial approximation distinct from series convergence in the wording.

## Part C — lesson

5–7 steps: prerequisites (higher derivatives, factorials); `e^x` about `0` with `T_1(1) = 2`, `T_3(1) = 8/3`, `T_6(1) = 1957/720`, prediction before the bound reveal; `sin x` and the bound `|x - c|^(n+1) / (n + 1)!`; `1/(1 - x)` outside the convergence interval, where raising the degree makes the error larger; the flat function: all coefficients vanish but `f(x) > 0` for `x > 0`; handoff. `sl`/`en` content, tests, smoke row.

## Acceptance criteria

- `e^x` about `0`: `T_1(1) = 2`, `T_3(1) = 8/3`, `T_6(1) = 1957/720`.
- The displayed bound is at least the actual absolute error for every example where it is shown, checked against independent reference values (not the same formula).
- `1/(1 - x)` about `0` at `x = -1.5` (outside `|x| < 1`): the error grows with the degree, so higher degree does not imply improvement everywhere.
- Flat example: `T_n = 0` for every `n` and `f(1) = e^(-1) > 0`.
- Controls and lesson actions produce equivalent configurations; presets round-trip; invalid data is rejected with an actionable error.

## Implementation notes

- **Model and vocabulary.** The model is `{example, center, degree, x, boundVisible}` (Part A). Closed vocabulary: parameters `taylor-center`, `taylor-degree`, `taylor-x`; visibility target `taylor-bound`; choice `taylor-example` (`exp`, `sine`, `geometric`, `flat`). `src/graphs/base/presets/PresetImport.ts` registers collection `taylor` with its own diagnosis: the example list, the per-example center range (and, for the flat function, "must be 0"), the per-example range of `x`, the integer degree `0..10` and the `boundVisible` flag each get a specific actionable message; wrong types keep the generic function-data message. Route `/analysis/taylor`, Analysis family entry (7 entries, 41 routes), lesson availability and a placeholder preview (`catalog/taylor.png`, a copy of the convexity placeholder; the Batch B release task regenerates previews).
- **Part A additions (small fixes to the committed maths and shared pieces).** `taylorCoefficients(example, center, degree)` and `evaluateTaylorCoefficients(coefficients, offset)` (Horner at any real offset, so the board draws `T_n` beyond the `x` domain; `taylorPolynomial` now uses them, same numbers); `formatSignificant` in `src/analysis/format.ts` (four significant digits, plain decimals from `0.0001` to `10^6`, `1,23·10⁻⁷` outside, and anything below `1e-12` shown as `0`, so `-0` and `1e-17` never reach the learner; tested in `format.test.ts`). `NumberInput` gained an optional `describedBy` (joined with its hint in `aria-describedby`) so the disabled flat-function center field points at its explanation. The committed mathematics (`taylor.ts`, `taylorModel.ts`) is otherwise unchanged; two tests were added to `taylor.test.ts` (32 tests).
- **Reject, never clamp.** `setCenter`, `setDegree`, `setX` (so also number fields, the degree buttons, lesson actions and drags) throw `DomainError` and leave the model unchanged for a value outside the example's own domain (the flat center only accepts `0`, `x` for `1/(1 - x)` stops at `0.95`, the degree must be an integer `0..10`). A drag is projected onto a `0.05` grid for the center and a `0.01` grid for `x` and clamped to the example's range; the flat center point cannot leave `0`. `setExample` loads the example's model (`TAYLOR_EXAMPLE_MODELS`) with `boundVisible = false` and switches to the example's own board view (`EXAMPLE_VIEWS`); a preset of another example without a stored view does the same. Editing the center, degree or `x` keeps the reveal.
- **Board.** Fixed pool of 13 objects whatever the example, degree or reveal: the convergence-interval segment and its label (visible only for `1/(1 - x)`), the translucent bound band, a dashed grey drop line at `c`, `f` (solid, green), `T_n` (dashed, blue, drawn with the coefficients as a plain polynomial so it also exists outside the `x` domain; `f` of `1/(1 - x)` is drawn left of `1` only), the error as a vertical segment at `x` between `T_n(x)` and `f(x)` (hidden when the error is exactly `0`), three value points, draggable `c` and `x` on the axis, one caption (top-left: formula, `c, n, x`, `T_n(x)` and `f(x)`, the absolute error, the bound line or "error bound: hidden", and a key "solid line: f; dashed line: T_n; vertical line at x: the error" on boards at least 520 px wide). `f` and `T_n` differ by pattern (solid versus dash 3) and are named in the inspector legend; they are not labelled on the board itself (the key line and the legend do that), which is a deviation from "pattern + label" that browser review should judge.
- **Readout ids.** `center`, `degree`, `x`, `polynomial`, `function`, `signed-error`, `absolute-error`, `bound`, `bound-hypothesis`, `convergence`. Errors and the bound use `formatSignificant`; `T_n(x)`, `f(x)`, `c` and `x` use four decimals. `bound` and `bound-hypothesis` read "skrito" / "hidden" until revealed; for the flat function the bound reads "ni prikazana (glej opombo)" / "not shown (see the note)". `bound-hypothesis` states `M` per example in words and formula (`max(e^c, e^x)` because `e^t` is increasing; `1` because every derivative of `sin x` is `±sin x` or `±cos x`; `(n+1)!/(1 - max(c, x))^(n+2)` because `f^(n+1)` increases on `x < 1`; none for the flat function). `convergence` states the series behavior in words: converges to `f` everywhere (`exp`, `sine`); for `1/(1 - x)` the interval `|x - c| < 1 - c` as numbers and whether `x` is inside; for the flat function that the series converges to `0` and not to `f`. Statuses (words, no changing numbers): `hidden` or `revealed`, `inside-convergence` or `outside-convergence` (`1/(1 - x)`), `no-bound` (flat, revealed), `flat`. The boundary `x = 2c - 1` counts as outside (the open interval).
- **Controls.** Example select; native number fields for `c` (disabled with a visible explanation and `aria-describedby` for the flat function), `x` and the degree; `-` and `+` buttons (`data-action="degree-decrease"` / `"degree-increase"`, `aria-label` in words, disabled at `0` and `10` with `aria-describedby="taylor-degree-help"`, whose text then names the limit); a native Show/Hide error bound button (`data-action="toggle-taylor-bound"`, `data-revealed`). The button group has its own label ("Spreminjanje stopnje") so it does not repeat the field's accessible name.
- **Lesson.** Seven steps (`taylor-guided`; frames 2 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 17): (1) `e^x` about `0`, `x = 1`, `n = 1`, bound hidden: `T_1(1) = 2`, `e - 2 = 0,7183`, prediction for `n = 3` and `n = 6`; (2) `n = 6` with the bound shown: `T_3(1) = 8/3`, `T_6(1) = 1957/720`, errors `0,0516` and `0,000226`, `M = max(e^c, e^x) = e < 3`, bound `e/7! = 0,000539`; (3) `sin x`, `x = 2`, `n = 3`, hidden, prediction; (4) revealed: `T_3(2) = 2/3`, `sin 2 = 0,9093`, error `0,2426`, `M = 1`, bound `2^4/4! = 2/3`, then `n = 5` (`T_5(2) = 14/15`, error `0,0240`, bound `4/45 = 0,0889`); (5) `1/(1 - x)` about `0` at `x = -1,5`, `n = 1`, hidden: the learner raises the degree; (6) recap of step 5 (`f - T_n = x^(n+1)/(1 - x)`, errors `0,9`, `2,025`, `4,556`; inside at `x = 0,5` `0,125` and `0,03125`) and the flat function at `x = 1`, hidden, prediction; (7) the flat reveal (`T_n(1) = 0`, `f(1) = e^(-1) = 0,3679`, series converges to `0`, no bound and why) and the handoff separating the finite polynomial from the series. Prediction steps (1, 3, 5, 6) hide the bound, ask the learner to predict and to change the degree or press the graph's own Show error bound, and carry no calculation frame; results are in `expectedObservations` and recapped in the next step.
