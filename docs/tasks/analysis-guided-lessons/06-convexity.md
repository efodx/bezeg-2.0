# 6. Convexity and concavity

**Source spec:** [Analysis guided lessons](../../specs/analysis-guided-lessons.md) — B2

**Dependencies:** Batch A released ([04](04-batch-a-release.md))

**Status:** implemented, not released

## Part A — mathematics (`src/analysis/convexity.ts`, `convexityModel.ts`)

- Named examples: `x^2` (default), `-x^2`, `x^3`, `x^4`, each with value, first and second derivative.
- Chord endpoints `a < b` and an independent tangent point `t`, all in a documented range (for example `[-2, 2]`).
- Second-derivative sign on an interval, computed analytically: `nonnegative`, `nonpositive`, or `changes` (`x^3` across `0`). `x^4` has `f''(0) = 0` and `f'' >= 0` everywhere, so it is convex with no inflection.
- Inflection points: points where `f''` changes sign (`x^3` at `0`; none for `x^4`).
- Comparisons on `[a, b]`: whether the graph lies below/above the chord, and whether it lies above/below the tangent at `t`. Claims are derived from the second-derivative hypothesis on the interval, not from sampling; when the sign changes on the interval, no claim is made.
- Model: `{example, a, b, t, secondDerivativeVisible}` validated by the parser (reject, never clamp).

## Part B — graph (`src/graphs/functions/ConvexityGraph.tsx`)

- Draggable chord endpoints and tangent point with native number fields; example select.
- Chord, tangent line, and a textual verdict for the interval `[a, b]` (convex, concave, or no claim with the reason). The tangent comparison is stated on `[a, b]` only when `t` lies in `[a, b]` and the hypothesis holds there.
- A **Show second derivative** reveal (visibility target) shows the sign of `f''` on the view (for example a sign strip or the `f''` graph) and the inflection point, if any.

## Part C — lesson

5–7 steps: prerequisites and the second derivative; `x^2` chord prediction then the inequality; tangent comparison; `-x^2` reverses both; `x^3` sign change and inflection at `0` (claims restricted to `[0, 2]` or `[-2, 0]`); `x^4` with `f''(0) = 0` and no inflection; handoff. `sl`/`en` content, tests, smoke row.

## Acceptance criteria

- Convex examples on an interval: graph below each chord, above each tangent with `t` in the interval; concave examples reverse both. Verified with an independent check (for example exact polynomial identities such as `f(x) - chord(x) = (x - a)(x - b)` for `x^2`).
- `x^3` on an interval containing `0` in its interior: the verdict is "no claim" with the sign change named; on `[0, 2]` it is convex.
- `x^4` on `[-1, 1]`: convex, `f''(0) = 0`, no inflection point.
- Controls and lesson actions produce equivalent configurations; presets round-trip; invalid data is rejected with an actionable error.

## Implementation notes

- **Model and vocabulary.** The model is `{example, a, b, t, secondDerivativeVisible}` (Part A). Closed vocabulary: parameters `convexity-a`, `convexity-b`, `convexity-t`; visibility target `second-derivative`; choice `convexity-example` (`square`, `negSquare`, `cube`, `quartic`). `src/graphs/base/presets/PresetImport.ts` registers collection `convexity` with its own diagnosis: the example list, the range `[-2, 2]` for `a`, `b` and `t`, `a < b` and the `secondDerivativeVisible` flag each get a specific actionable message; wrong types keep the generic function-data message. Route `/analysis/convexity`, Analysis family entry (6 entries, 40 routes), lesson availability and a placeholder preview (`catalog/convexity.png`, a copy of the extrema placeholder; the Batch B release task regenerates previews).
- **Part A additions (small fixes to the committed maths).** `ConvexityExample.secondDerivativeZeros` (all zeros of `f''`: `[]`, `[]`, `[0]`, `[0]`, a superset of the inflection points so that `x^4` at `0` is a zero without a sign change) and `secondDerivativePieces(example, from, to)` (the view cut at those zeros, each piece with its strict sign taken from the closed-form `secondDerivativeSign`, never sampled); `formatLineEquation` in `src/analysis/format.ts` ("y = 0,5x + 1,5", true minus, decimal comma). Tested in `convexity.test.ts` (23 tests).
- **Reject, never clamp.** `setA`, `setB`, `setT` (so also number fields, lesson actions and drags) throw `DomainError` and leave the model unchanged for a value outside `[-2, 2]` or `a >= b`; `t` is independent of `a` and `b` (any value in `[-2, 2]`). A drag of `a` or `b` is clamped to one grid step (`0.01`) short of the other endpoint, a drag of `t` to the range. `setExample` loads the example's model (`CONVEXITY_EXAMPLE_MODELS`) with `secondDerivativeVisible = false` and switches to the example's own board view (`EXAMPLE_VIEWS`; the bottom margin of `-x^2` is tall because the caption sits bottom-right); a preset of another example without a stored view does the same. Editing `a`, `b` or `t` keeps the reveal.
- **Board.** Fixed pool of 18 objects whatever the example: three dashed grey drop lines, the function, the chord (solid blue segment over `[a, b]`), the tangent (dashed amber line, `dash 3`), three small value points on the graph, one inflection ring plus its label (revealed only, at `f`'s inflection point anywhere in the range, not only inside `[a, b]`), two sign texts and one zero mark in a row at the top of the view (revealed only: bold `+`, `−` per piece of the view and `0` where `f''` vanishes), draggable `a`, `b` and `t` on the x-axis (`t` is on the axis, not on the graph, so it stays reachable when `f(t)` is off the view), one caption. Caption lines: formula, `a`, `b`, `t`, the verdict ("sodba: skrita" / "verdict: hidden" until revealed, then convex/concave/no claim with the reason), the position of the graph relative to chord and tangent (revealed and a claim exists; names the withdrawn tangent claim when `t` is outside `[a, b]`), a key "solid line: chord; dashed line: tangent" and, revealed, the sign key (both keys only on boards at least 520 px wide).
- **Readout ids.** `a`, `b`, `t`, `chord`, `tangent` (line equations, always shown), `second-derivative-sign`, `verdict`, `tangent-verdict`, `inflection` (the word "skrito" / "hidden" until revealed). Statuses (no changing numbers, except the fixed zero of `f''`): `hidden` or `revealed`, `no-claim` (`f''` changes sign on `[a, b]`), `tangent-outside` (verdict exists but `t` is not in `[a, b]`), `zero-without-change` (`x^4`: `f''` is `0` at `0` without a sign change).
- **Show second derivative.** A native button (`data-action="toggle-second-derivative"`, `data-revealed`), label flips to Hide; the inspector help asks the learner to predict first. The chord and the tangent are always drawn; the verdicts, the sign strip and the inflection point are part of the reveal.
- **Lesson.** Seven steps (`convexity-guided`; frames 2 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 3): (1) `x^2` on `[-1, 1.5]`, `t = 0.5`, hidden, prediction; (2) reveal with the worked example: chord `y = 0,5x + 1,5`, `f(x) - chord(x) = (x + 1)(x - 1,5) <= 0`, `f'' = 2 >= 0`; (3) tangent `y = x - 0,25`, `f(x) - tangent(x) = (x - 0,5)^2 >= 0`, then the learner moves `t` to `1,8` (outside `[a, b]`): claim withdrawn although `(x - 1,8)^2 >= 0` still happens to hold; (4) `-x^2` hidden, prediction; (5) `x^3` on `[-1, 1.5]` hidden, prediction, then the learner sets `[0, 2]` (convex) and `[-2, 0]` (concave); the explanation recaps `-x^2` (chord `y = -0,5x - 1,5`, tangent `y = -x + 0,25`, differences `-(x + 1)(x - 1,5)` and `-(x - 0,5)^2`); (6) `x^4` on `[-1, 1]` hidden, prediction; the explanation recaps `x^3` (chord `y = 1,75x + 0,75`, `f - chord = (x + 1)(x + 0,5)(x - 1,5)`, positive on `(-1, -0,5)` and negative on `(-0,5, 1,5)`, so no claim); (7) reveal with the worked example (`f'' = 12x^2`, `f''(0) = 0`, chord `y = 1`, tangent `y = 0,5x - 0,1875` with difference `(x - 0,5)^2 (x^2 + x + 0,75) >= 0`) and the handoff. Prediction steps (1, 4, 5, 6) hide the second derivative, ask the learner to predict and then press the graph's own Show second derivative, and carry no calculation frame; results are in `expectedObservations` and recapped in the next step.
